Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Shear Genius ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit our website or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — your name, phone number, and email address when you submit a contact form or reach out to us directly.
  • Usage data — general information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited and browser type, collected through standard web analytics.
  • Communications — records of messages and inquiries you send to us.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and appointment requests
  • Send appointment reminders and service-related notifications
  • Send promotional messages, special offers, and updates — only if you have opted in to receive them
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. SMS / Text Message Communications

By providing your phone number and checking the applicable consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive text messages from Shear Genius. We offer two types of SMS communications:

3a. Non-Marketing SMS (Service Notifications)

By consenting, you agree to receive non-marketing text messages from Shear Genius about appointment confirmations, reminders, and other service-related notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

3b. Marketing SMS (Promotional Messages)

By consenting, you agree to receive marketing and promotional messages — including special offers, discounts, and new service updates — from Shear Genius at the phone number provided. Frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply.

To get help: Text HELP to any message you receive from us.

To opt out: Text STOP at any time to unsubscribe from any SMS program. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist in operating our website and business (such as our CRM and messaging platform), subject to confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at s.genius10@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to improve your browsing experience. You can configure your browser to refuse cookies, though some features of the site may not function correctly as a result.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
